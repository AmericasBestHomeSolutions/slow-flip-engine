## 2. Required risk disclosure (must appear with any offer material)

| ID | Snippet |
|---|---|
| **LL-10** | Private lending involves risk, including possible loss of principal. Notes are not bank deposits and are not insured by the FDIC or any government agency. Past performance does not guarantee future results. |
| **LL-11** | Your investment is illiquid for the note term. Do not invest funds you may need before the note matures. |
| **LL-12** | This is not an offer to sell securities. Any offer is made only through the note documents, to qualified investors, in accordance with applicable securities laws. |

Placement rules: LL-10 + LL-12 on the one-pager, pitch deck, and any document
that states the rate. LL-11 delivered verbally in every pitch (it's in the
script) and printed in the process section.

## 3. Prohibited language (never, anywhere, in any channel)

- "Guaranteed," "risk-free," "safe as a CD," "can't lose," "no risk"
- Any projected return beyond the stated note terms
- "SEC-approved," "SEC-registered" (exemption ≠ approval)
- Comparisons implying bank-deposit safety or insurance
- Urgency mechanics on the offer itself ("only 3 spots," "closes Friday")
- Any rate or performance number not sourced from LL-01/LL-02/LL-06

## 4. Testimonial & spotlight rules

- Written consent on file before any investor is named or quoted
- Quotes describe **experience** (communication, punctual payments, process) —
  never returns beyond LL-01 language, never "you should invest"
- Every testimonial is presented with: *"Individual experience; not a promise
  of future performance."* (**LL-20**)
- No compensated testimonials without disclosure
