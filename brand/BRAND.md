**The movement where everybody eats.**

This is the master brand document for Lonchando. The visual board that goes with it

## 1. The Name

**Lonchando** — from *lonchar*, Dominican street Spanish for *to eat*.

- **Lonchar** = to eat. To eat good. To get paid. To win.
- **Lonchando** = present progressive — *eating, right now, in motion.*

The name is a status report: we are not going to eat someday. **We are eating now,
and the table has room.** The present-progressive "-ando" is the whole brand in one
suffix — the movement never stops moving.

Spelling is always **L-O-N-C-H-A-N-D-O**. Never "Lonchado," never anglicized.

## 2. What Lonchando Is

Lonchando is the platform where regular people learn to build wealth the slow,
certain way — and everybody at the table eats.

Three things live under one roof:

1. **The Engine** — the slow flip real estate machine (this repo's Lead Engine is
   its operating tool). Buy right, hold steady, let time do the heavy lifting.
2. **The Table** — investors who fund the work earn **12% fixed interest**. That's
   El Doce. It is the proof that the movement pays, not just preaches.
3. **The Voice** — mindset, music, and message. Napoleon Hill, Earl Nightingale,
   Joe Dispenza on the mind. Call-and-response, Dominican soul, gospel-cookout
   energy on the mic. The songs, the chants, the positivity — that's not
   decoration, that IS the marketing.

**Positioning statement:** *For working people who were told wealth wasn't for
them, Lonchando is the movement that proves it is — with a real engine (slow flip
real estate), a real number (12% to investors), and a real rhythm (music, mindset,
and a table where everybody eats).*

### Brand architecture: Lonchando × IronStone Legacy

Two brands, two floors of the same house — neither replaces the other:

| | **LONCHANDO** | **IRONSTONE LEGACY** |
|---|---|---|
| Role | The movement | The institution |
| Volume | Loud | Quiet |
| Audience | The public — community, followers, culture | Investors — private, documented relationships |
| Carries | Merch, music, mindset, events, content | Note agreements, statements, quarterly letters, the wire confirmation |
| Voice | Preacher at the cookout | Banker who keeps his word |
| Colors | Verde on Noche | Restrained — IronStone gets its own quiet system (steel/charcoal/oro accent) when its collateral is built |

The split is also the compliance architecture: **Lonchando markets the movement
publicly and never contains an offer; IronStone Legacy makes offers privately and
never goes out to strangers.** The funnel between them is specified in
[`capital/RAISE-SYSTEM.md`](../capital/RAISE-SYSTEM.md).

## 4. Voice & Tone

**The preacher at the cookout.** Warm like family, direct like a coach, certain
like a man who has the receipts.

- **Direct.** Short sentences. Say the number. "12%. Fixed. Every year."
- **Celebratory.** We celebrate other people's wins louder than our own.
- **Bilingual by nature.** Spanglish is not a gimmick, it's the accent of the
  audience. Lead lines can go either language; the chant is always Spanish.
- **Call-and-response.** The audience is never spectating. Every talk, every
  video, every caption invites the answer back.
- **Never hype, always proof.** We don't say "get rich." We say "here's the
  deal, here's the number, here's the man who got paid last month."

**La Escalera** is pure geometry (rectangles only) — it embroiders, vinyl-cuts,
and scales down to a favicon with zero loss. The three steps rise in equal
increments: that's the slow flip. Steady. No spikes.

**Wordmark rules:** always all-caps, Bebas Neue, generous letter-spacing.
Clear space around the mark = the height of the letter O on all sides.
Never stretch, never outline, never gradient, never rotate.
(Before sending wordmark SVGs to a printer, convert text to outlines.)

### Color

| Name | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Noche** | `#0a0a0a` | The ground. The brand lives on black. |
| **Verde Lonchando** | `#c8f542` | The voice. The signature. Use it loud but singular. |
| **Oro Doce** | `#f5a623` | The money color. Reserved for El Doce / 12% moments. |

## 6. El Doce — Marketing the 12%

The number is the movement's proof. Market it like a scoreboard, not a pitch:

- Say it plainly: **"Investors in our notes earn 12% fixed annual interest."**
- Show receipts: monthly "El Doce paid" posts, investor spotlights (with consent).
- Tie it to the method: 12% is affordable to pay *because* the slow flip works.
- The badge (12 ticks = 12 months) goes on every investor-facing document.

### Market it right (important)

Raising money from investors is a regulated activity. Build the brand loud, make
the offers carefully:

- **Market the movement publicly; make offers privately.** The public brand sells
  education, community, music, merch. Actual investment offers should go through
  your proper channel (your attorney-reviewed note docs / exemption structure —
  e.g., Reg D 506(b) relationships or 506(c) accredited-only with verification).
- Say **"12% fixed interest per the note agreement"** — never "guaranteed,"
  never "risk-free," never projected returns beyond the note terms.
- Keep testimonials factual and compliant; get it in writing.
- Run merch, music, and mindset content freely — that's the funnel's top and it
  has no securities strings attached.

*(Not legal advice — have your securities attorney bless the investor-facing
templates once, then reuse them.)*
